      Common questions

      Will OTPs from any site arrive here?

      The inbox receives real verification and OTP mail for sign-ups, shopping sites, apps, games and trials. If one domain is refused by a site, switch to another of the 8 domains and create a fresh address — that solves most rejections. Large banks and a few big platforms (for example Google or Facebook) block all public temp-mail domains on their side, so no temp-mail service can promise those.

      How many addresses can I make?

      Unlimited. Create as many as you need, on any of the 8 domains, and switch between them from “My mailboxes”. There is no daily cap.

      What does the 30-day keep actually do?

      Reserving stores the address in this browser for 30 days from the moment you reserve it, with a live countdown and expiry date. Guerrilla Mail deletes mail on its own servers after about an hour, so for reserved addresses this page also keeps its own copy of every received mail — that copy is what stays readable for the full 30 days. Because the storage is this browser on this device, use the same browser to come back to a reserved mail.

      Can I send email from these addresses?

      No — these are receive-only inboxes, made for sign-ups and verification codes. That is also what keeps them fast and spam-free.

      What this service does

      Temp Mail VIP creates receive-only temporary email addresses through third-party mail providers (Guerrilla Mail, Maildrop and Mail.tm) and shows incoming verification and OTP mail in your browser. We do not operate our own mail server and we cannot send mail from these addresses.

      Information stored in your browser

      • Addresses you create, and — only for addresses you reserve — a copy of received mail, are stored in your own browser on your own device (browser local storage) so a reserved address can stay available for its 30 days.
      • That stored data is not sent to us and we have no account system, no sign-up form and no server database for your inbox.
      • Clearing your browser data, or using a different browser or device, removes access to locally stored reserved addresses and their mail copies.
      • The mail providers delete mail on their own servers on their own schedules (for example, Guerrilla Mail keeps mail for about an hour); delivery and retention by those providers are outside our control.
