      Common questions

      Will OTPs from any site arrive here?

      The inbox receives real verification and OTP mail for sign-ups, shopping sites, apps, games and trials. If one domain is refused by a site, switch to another of the 6 domains and create a fresh address — that solves most rejections. Large banks and a few big platforms (for example Google or Facebook) block all public temp-mail domains on their side, so no temp-mail service can promise those.

      How many addresses can I make?

      Unlimited. Create as many as you need, on any of the 6 domains, and switch between them from “My mailboxes”. There is no daily cap.

      What does the 30-day keep actually do?

      Reserving stores the address in this browser for 30 days from the moment you reserve it, with a live countdown and expiry date. Guerrilla Mail deletes mail on its own servers after about an hour, so for reserved addresses this page also keeps its own copy of every received mail — that copy is what stays readable for the full 30 days. Because the storage is this browser on this device, use the same browser to come back to a reserved mail.

      Can I send email from these addresses?

      No — these are receive-only inboxes, made for sign-ups and verification codes. That is also what keeps them fast and spam-free.
